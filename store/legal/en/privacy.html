What stays on your phone

The app stores in your phone's (or browser's) storage: your settings (level, preferences, theme, language), your saved routes and favourites, your ski diary with the GPS points of your ski days, the location of your hotel or car, runs you closed yourself, your group name and group code, your photos, your weight (if you enter it), the downloaded ski areas and, if you have Pro, your licence code. This data is not sent anywhere. You can erase it via Menu > Storage and privacy, or by deleting the app.

Your location

If you give permission, the app uses your phone's GPS to show you on the map, to navigate, to keep your ski diary and to show your speed. That happens on your phone. Your location only goes to others if you are in a group and "Share my position" is on (see below).

Apple Health (iPhone app)

If you turn on "Save to Apple Health", your iPhone first asks for permission. After a ski day the app saves it as a workout in Apple Health: the start and end time, the distance and an estimate of the calories. For that estimate you can enter your weight; it stays on your phone. The app reads nothing from Apple Health and does not send this data anywhere. Health data is never used for advertising.

Photos, motion sensor and Apple Watch

Photos you take or choose for your ski day are stored only on your phone. The app uses the motion sensor while recording a ski day to recognise jumps; that also happens on your phone. If you have the Apple Watch app, your iPhone sends your ski day and route directly to your watch. Widgets and the Live Activity show data that is already on your phone. While you navigate, the Watch starts a workout session so it can tap your wrist at a turn even with your arm down; that session is not saved to Health and the app reads no health data for it. You can turn this off in the settings.

Downloaded ski areas

Before you use a ski area, you download it: the run data and the elevation data for the winter map and 3D are then stored on your phone, so everything works without signal. You erase them via Menu > Storage and privacy.

Ads in the iPhone app

In the free iPhone app we show ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited). On first use a message from Google asks for your consent for personalised ads, and then your iPhone asks whether Skipilot may track you. If you refuse, you get ads that are not tailored to you. You change your choice with Google in the app via Menu → Ad privacy settings, and with Apple via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. More about AdMob: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Ads and cookies

To keep Skipilot free, the website and the app show ads from Google AdSense (Google Ireland Limited). On your first visit a message from Google asks whether you consent to cookies and personalised ads. If you do not consent, you get ads that are not tailored to you; Google then uses only what is needed to show ads and prevent abuse.

You can always change your choice via the button or shield Google shows at the bottom of the page, or by clearing this website's cookies in your browser. More about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Legal basis

The data is used to make the app work as you ask (performance of the agreement) and, for a purchase, to meet the legal record-keeping obligation. For personalised ads in the iPhone app, your consent is the legal basis. For cookies and personalised ads, your consent is the legal basis. For your location, your phone first asks for permission; you can always withdraw it in your phone's settings.

How long

Data on your phone stays until you erase it. Group messages disappear from ntfy.sh after a short time. Order data is kept for seven years, because tax law requires it.
