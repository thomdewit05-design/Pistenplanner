Safety first. Skipilot is a guide. Run data comes from volunteers (OpenStreetMap) and may contain errors or be out of date. The app does not know which runs and lifts are actually open, has no avalanche information, and times are estimates.

Always follow the signs, the closures and the instructions of the ski patrol. Only ski runs that match your level. In an accident, call the emergency number.
