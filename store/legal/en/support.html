Frequently asked questions

How do I plan a route?
Choose a ski area and download it. Then tap a run to ski it, or tap two points on the map (start and destination). Or take today's day route.

Does the app work without signal?
Yes. You download a ski area once with internet; after that the map, 3D and navigation also work without signal on the mountain.

Is that lift or run really closed?
The app does not know which runs and lifts are actually open today. Under Open and closed you mark what is closed yourself; routes take it into account. Always follow the signs and the ski patrol.

A run is missing, or something on the map is wrong.
The runs come from OpenStreetMap, a map maintained by volunteers. Email which ski area and which run it is; then it can be added at the source.

I get no spoken directions.
Turn on Spoken directions in the menu, and check that your iPhone's sound is on and not muted.

Navigation on my Apple Watch?
Start navigation on your iPhone and lock the screen. Your Apple Watch shows the next turn; before every turn you feel a tap on your wrist.

Why do I see ads?
Skipilot is free thanks to ads. They never appear during navigation. You can change your choice about personalised ads in the menu.

My data
Your routes and ski diary stay on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy and the terms.
